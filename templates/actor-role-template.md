## Playable role sheet

- Objective: one active, scene-playable aim
- Knowledge: what this character can credibly notice or contribute
- Relationships: one invitation and one productive friction point
- Secret / unanswered question: something that can shape choices without
  becoming certainty
- Boundary: what the character must not decide, narrate, or override for others
- Play style: behavioral texture, not a generic personality adjective
- Voice guidance: concrete language habits, sensory register, and rhythm

- Did their knowledge create an action, question, relationship beat, or cost?
